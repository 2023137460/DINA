Come home at 7:00 PM to a dinner that is tender, evenly thawed, and ready for the pan.

DINA is an upright 60 cm companion freezer concept with an independent defrost drawer and motorized carousel. Load meals on Sunday; DINA lowers tonight’s portion through a thermal airlock and gently thaws it for dinner.

Two thermally isolated zones in one kitchen appliance. Grab tonight’s thawed dinner from the lower drawer without opening the main freezer door — keeping 110 L of frozen food locked at −18 °C.

Volume Math: 185 L total = 110 L upper shelves (−18 °C) + 60 L motorized carousel + 15 L insulated defrost drawer.

Carousel Math: 11 meals = two stacked discs (6 containers upper + 5 lower), plus 1 empty pass-through sector so a meal from the upper disc can drop through the lower disc and the 2.5 s airlock hatch into the drawer.

−18 °C deep freeze

Upper Freezer & Meal Vault

Holds 110 L of standard freezer shelves for groceries, vegetables, and ice cream, directly above the 60 L motorized carousel holding 11 meals at −18 °C. The upper cabinet door stays shut at dinnertime, preventing warm air infiltration and saving energy.

+3.0 °C defrost drawer

Independent Defrost Drawer

Operates closed-loop active thaw: forced-air convection at +10 °C runs only until IR/NTC surface telemetry reads +4.0 °C. The chamber then steps down to a +3.0 °C hold while the core equalizes by conduction, keeping the meal fresh for up to 24 hours.

Amber: Active Thaw (+10 °C air) Green: Ready to Cook (+3.0 °C hold) Blue: Standby / −18 °C Vault

Forgot to defrost dinner?

Dinner is the most challenging meal to make a decision on for 57% of households (Dolmio / OnePoll 2024 survey of 2,000 UK adults, which found people spend an average of 43 minutes a week deliberating, with dinnertime stress starting as early as 15:10). When you forget to take food out of the freezer the night before, weeknight options fail.

Microwaves overheat thin outer edges into gray rubber while the center stays frozen solid. Leaving meat on the kitchen counter sits for hours in the bacterial danger zone (+5 °C to +60 °C). Takeaway delivery costs 35 € while wholesome groceries already paid for sit frozen.

DINA eliminates the weeknight compromise: stock 11 prepped meals on Sunday, confirm dinner from your phone, and return home to food thawed evenly under closed-loop surface temperature control.

Questions & Answers

How easy is it to keep clean? Your food stays sealed inside BPA-free 750 ml containers that go straight into the dishwasher (compliant with EU Regulation 10/2011). The interior drum wipes clean with a damp cloth in seconds, and defrost moisture channels into a self-evaporating system powered by compressor waste heat, with a removable pan for periodic cleaning.

Will I hear it in an open-plan kitchen? With an unvalidated design target under 32 dBA, DINA is engineered to operate below conversational volume. The revolving carousel turns for only 2.5 seconds when loading groceries or dropping dinner.

Does thawing dinner warm the rest of the freezer? Thermal transfer is prevented under normal operation by closed-loop surface limiting and a 25 mm vacuum insulation panel (VIP) separating the −18 °C upper vault from the lower drawer. The motorized airlock hatch opens for only 2.5 seconds during drop transfer, keeping internal freezer temperatures stable.

What happens if the power goes out? DINA logs internal temperature telemetry throughout. If power returns and the meal remained safely chilled, the cycle safely resumes. If temperature telemetry records food warming above +5.0 °C for more than 2 hours, DINA halts active warming and triggers a phone alert recommending discarding the dish (conforming to EFSA temperature abuse guidelines).

Does DINA require Wi-Fi to operate? No. Your weekly meal schedule is stored locally on the appliance. Tonight’s meal drops and thaws on time even if your home internet is temporarily disconnected.

What warranty and support is included? For coursework evaluation, the design assumes a comprehensive 2-year European warranty with modular, repairable components (an illustrative go-to-market assumption). Scheduling, commute sync, and smart home alerts require no monthly fee.
