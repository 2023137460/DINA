Compromise 01 • The Microwave Cooked Edges

Rubbery Edges, Icy Center

  • Microwaves overheat thin edges until they turn gray and tough while the middle stays frozen solid.
  • Ruins the tenderness and juices of good meat, fish, or poultry before it even hits the pan.
Compromise 02 • The Countertop Food Safety Risk

Hours in a Warm Kitchen

  • Leaving food out on the counter or in warm water warms the outside into the +5 to +60 °C bacterial danger zone.
  • The surface sits warm for hours while you wait for the center to soften.
Compromise 03 • Takeaway Apps Extra Expense

Another Unplanned Takeaway

  • Paying for a typical delivery order plus service fees just because dinner is still frozen solid in the freezer.
  • You wait 45 minutes for lukewarm takeout while the groceries you already paid for stay unused.

Power-Outage Protection

What Happens If the Power Goes Out While You’re Away?

You never have to guess if your food is still safe. When power comes back on, DINA checks how long the outage lasted and how cold the drawer stayed. If the interruption was brief and your dinner stayed safely chilled, thawing finishes normally. If the food surface ever warmed above +5.0 °C for more than 2 hours, DINA stops thawing, glows red on the handle, and sends a clear alert to your phone to discard that meal.

Works Without Wi-Fi

What If Your Home Internet Drops or a Dish Is in the Drawer?

Your dinner never depends on your Wi-Fi router. DINA remembers your weekly schedule right on the appliance—dropping tonight’s meal on time and holding it at +3.0 °C (up to 24 h) even if your internet is offline. And if the lower drawer is left open or still has yesterday’s container inside, the drop hatch stays safely closed and sends a reminder to your phone.

Questions & Answers

Living with DINA

Straightforward answers on everyday cleaning, kitchen quietness, energy efficiency, and warranty coverage.

01 • Easy Cleaning

How easy is it to keep clean?

Your food stays inside sealed, BPA-free 750 ml containers that go straight into the dishwasher after dinner. The smooth interior and carousel wipe clean with a damp cloth. Any moisture from defrosting collects in a self-evaporating drip tray that slides out easily and is 100% dishwasher-safe, with gentle app reminders when it’s time for a quick rinse.

02 • Kitchen Quietness

Will I hear it in an open-plan kitchen?

Barely a whisper. At under 32 dBA, DINA is quieter than a soft conversation or a library room. The inner meal carousel turns for just a few seconds when you load groceries or when tonight’s meal drops into the drawer while you’re at work.

03 • Energy & Cold Protection

Does thawing dinner warm up the rest of the freezer?

Not at all. A thick vacuum-insulated barrier separates the −18 °C freezer above from the lower defrost drawer, and the internal drop hatch opens for just 2.5 seconds. Even better, because you only slide open the small lower drawer at dinnertime, you don’t let cold air spill out of the main freezer door every evening.

04 • Warranty & Zero Subscriptions

What warranty is included, and is there a monthly fee?

DINA comes with a full 2-year European consumer warranty and is built with easy-to-service modular parts. Most importantly, your kitchen appliance doesn’t come with a bill: core app features (scheduling, afternoon check-ins, commute sync, and smart-home alerts) are free with no subscription.
