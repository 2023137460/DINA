Workflow

How it works

From Sunday meal preparation to tonight’s dinner, safely automated in three steps.

01

Load & Tag

Open the upper door and insert your prepared containers into the front loading aperture. In the companion app, tag the meal category and portion weight. DINA indexes the two-tier carousel and stores each meal at −18.0 °C.

02

Gravity Drop

At the calculated thermodynamic trigger hour, the stepper motor aligns the selected meal sector and pass-through drop chimney (“espaço vazio”). The motorized thermal airlock opens for 2.5 seconds, dropping the container into the lower bay.

03

Thaw & Safe-Hold

A reversible Peltier heat pump warms the drawer with convective circulation at +10.0 °C. The moment surface thermistors register +4.0 °C, heating halts immediately and locks into continuous +3.0 °C Chilled Safe-Hold until served.

Food Science & HACCP

Defrost science & safety

Eliminating the dangerous +5 °C to +60 °C microbial danger zone without microwave textural damage.

The Danger Zone Barrier

Leaving meat to thaw on kitchen countertops exposes outer layers to +20 °C to +25 °C ambient air for hours, causing rapid microbial proliferation (Listeria, Salmonella). Microwave defrost par-cooks cellular borders and dries out juices. DINA enforces a closed-loop convective cycle at +10 °C that preserves cellular texture.

+3.0 °C Chilled Safe-Hold

The instant container surface thermistors read +4.0 °C, DINA reverses the Peltier heat pump into cooling mode. The drawer stabilizes at +3.0 °C to +4.0 °C, complying with DIN 10508 and FDA Food Code perishable chilled storage standards indefinitely.

RGB LED Halo Handle

The brushed aluminum drawer handle features an integrated circular light ring that communicates system status at a glance: Pulsing Cyan for freezing, Pulsing Amber during active thaw, Solid Green for ready and chilled at +3.0 °C, and Flashing Red for power outage alert.

Software & IoT

DINA Mobile OS

Native Matter over Wi-Fi integration and local ESP32-S3 schedule resilience.

Connected Kitchen Platform

DINA connects directly to Apple Home, Google Home, Samsung SmartThings, and Home Assistant via Matter over Wi-Fi. Advanced dinner scheduling, portion tagging, and thermodynamic calculations run locally on the appliance.

  • Local NVS Persistence: All dinner drop times and thaw parameters are cached onboard in ESP32-S3 non-volatile memory. Wi-Fi disconnection never delays a scheduled dinner.
  • Commute Sync: Integrates with transit schedules and GPS traffic APIs to push the thaw trigger or arm +3.0 °C Chilled Safe-Hold if you are delayed in transit.
  • Power Outage Telemetry: In the event of a power cut, onboard thermistor logs verify safe temperature envelopes upon restoration.
