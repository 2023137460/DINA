Power-Outage Behaviour

Blackout Telemetry & 2-Hour Safety Rule

When power returns after an outage, DINA checks elapsed blackout time and surface temperature logs stored in non-volatile memory. If the meal stayed safely chilled, thawing resumes automatically. If the food surface exceeded +5.0 °C for more than 2 hours cumulatively, heating is locked out, the handle halo flashes red, and the app alerts you to discard the meal.

Wi-Fi Offline Autonomy

Onboard Schedule Execution & Interlocks

All scheduled drop times and thaw parameters are cached locally on the appliance controller. If home Wi-Fi or your phone goes offline, DINA executes your scheduled drop on time and transitions automatically into +3.0 °C Safe-Hold (up to 24 h). If the lower drawer is open or already holds a container at drop time, the thermal airlock stays sealed and notifies you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical questions, answered

Everything you need to know about day-to-day cleaning, kitchen acoustics, energy efficiency, and warranty support.

01 • Cleaning & Hygiene

How do I clean the containers, drum, and condensate dish?

Meals sit inside sealed 750 ml BPA-free polypropylene containers that go straight into the dishwasher. The revolving POM-C drum and sanitary HIPS interior liners wipe clean easily. Defrost moisture drains into a warm evaporation dish above the compressor that evaporates water passively; because raw-food drip can occur, the dish is removable, dishwasher-safe, and easy to wipe, with periodic cleaning reminders in the app.

02 • Noise Level

How quiet is DINA in an open-plan kitchen?

DINA operates at under 32 dBA thanks to a variable-speed inverter compressor and brushless micro-convection fan. The motorized carousel turns briefly for a few seconds only when you load a container or when a scheduled drop is triggered.

03 • Energy Use & Insulation

Does thawing in the lower drawer warm up the freezer above?

No. The −18 °C drum chamber and the lower defrost drawer are separated by a 48 mm hybrid Vacuum Insulation Panel (VIP) and polyurethane barrier, paired with a motorized dual-seal silicone airlock hatch that opens for only 2.5 seconds during a drop. Because the lower drawer slides out independently, you never open the main freezer door to retrieve dinner.

04 • Warranty & Subscriptions

What warranty is included, and is there a monthly fee?

DINA is designed to include a full 2-year European consumer hardware warranty backed by modular, serviceable subassemblies. Core companion app features (meal tagging, scheduling, commute sync, safety alerts) and Matter smart-home integration are free with no subscription.
