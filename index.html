Upper Freezer & Meal Vault

Holds 110 L of standard freezer shelves for groceries, vegetables, and ice cream, directly above the 60 L motorized carousel holding 11 meals at −18 °C. The upper cabinet door stays shut at dinnertime, preventing warm air infiltration and saving energy.

+3.0 °C defrost drawer

Independent Defrost Drawer

Operates closed-loop active thaw: forced-air convection at +10 °C runs only until IR/NTC surface telemetry reads +4.0 °C. The chamber then steps down to a +3.0 °C hold while the core equalizes by conduction, keeping the meal fresh for up to 24 hours.

Questions & Answers

How easy is it to keep clean? Your food stays sealed inside BPA-free 750 ml containers that go straight into the dishwasher (compliant with EU Regulation 10/2011). The interior drum wipes clean with a damp cloth in seconds, and defrost moisture channels into a removable, dishwasher-safe condensate collection tray that slides out from below the drawer for effortless periodic rinsing.

Will I hear it in an open-plan kitchen? With an unvalidated design target under 32 dBA, DINA is engineered to operate below conversational volume. The revolving carousel turns for only 2.5 seconds when loading groceries or dropping dinner.

Does thawing dinner warm the rest of the freezer? Thermal transfer is prevented under normal operation by closed-loop surface limiting and a 25 mm vacuum insulation panel (VIP) separating the −18 °C upper vault from the lower drawer. The motorized airlock hatch opens for only 2.5 seconds during drop transfer, keeping internal freezer temperatures stable.

What happens if the power goes out? DINA logs internal temperature telemetry throughout. If power returns within 30 minutes and the meal remained safely chilled below +5.0 °C, the cycle safely resumes. If temperature telemetry records food warming above +5.0 °C for more than 30 minutes, DINA triggers a Tier 1 conservative warning and pauses heating (accounting for thermal lag and sensor latency). If food temperature exceeds +5.0 °C for more than 2 hours, DINA permanently halts active warming and triggers a phone alert recommending discarding the dish (conforming to USDA / EFSA temperature abuse guidelines).

Does DINA require Wi-Fi to operate? No. Your weekly meal schedule is stored locally on the appliance. Tonight’s meal drops and thaws on time even if your home internet is temporarily disconnected.

What warranty and support is included? For coursework evaluation, the design assumes a comprehensive 2-year European warranty with modular, repairable components (an illustrative go-to-market assumption). Scheduling, commute sync, and smart home alerts require no monthly fee.
