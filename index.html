Questions & Answers

How easy is it to keep clean? Your food stays sealed inside BPA-free 750 ml containers that go straight into the dishwasher. The interior drum wipes clean with a damp cloth in seconds, and any defrost moisture evaporates into an easy-rinse tray.

Will I hear it in an open-plan kitchen? At under 32 dBA, DINA operates below conversational volume. The revolving carousel turns for only 2.5 seconds when loading groceries or dropping dinner.

Does thawing dinner warm the rest of the freezer? Never. A thick vacuum insulation barrier isolates the −18 °C upper cabinet from the lower drawer, and the internal drop hatch opens for just 2.5 seconds. The main freezer door stays completely closed.

What happens if the power goes out? DINA logs internal temperatures throughout. If power returns quickly and dinner stayed safely cold, thawing completes. If food warmed above +5.0 °C for more than 2 hours, DINA halts and alerts your phone to discard the dish.

Does DINA require Wi-Fi to operate? No. Your weekly meal schedule is stored locally on the appliance. Tonight’s meal drops and thaws on time even if your home internet is temporarily disconnected.

What warranty and support is included? Every unit includes a comprehensive 2-year European warranty with modular, repairable components. Scheduling, commute sync, and smart home alerts require no monthly fee.
