The 4:30 PM Dilemma

“Shit. I forgot to defrost dinner…”

Have you ever been at your desk at 4:30 PM and had that sinking feeling? Tonight’s healthy dinner plan collapses before you even leave the office. Now you are stuck choosing between three terrible compromises:

Option 01 • The Microwave

The Gray Rubber Boot

Four minutes on the “Defrost” button par-cooks gray crusts, wrings out natural moisture, and turns premium steak or chicken into dry rubber — while the center stays an iceberg. Good food ruined in four minutes.

Option 02 • The Countertop

The Science Experiment

Submerging a vacuum pack into a sink of lukewarm tap water like a sketchy chemistry project. The outer surface sits in the bacterial danger zone for an hour while you stand around waiting for uneven results.

Option 03 • The Delivery App

Overpriced Takeout. Again.

Admitting defeat, opening a delivery app, and spending €25 on greasy, sodium-heavy takeout — while the fresh, high-quality groceries you carefully bought sit frozen and untouched in your freezer.

The Routine

A new kind of kitchen routine

From Sunday meal prep to a freshly thawed dinner, effortlessly orchestrated in three calm steps.

01

Load on Sunday

Open the upper door and slide your portion containers into the rotating carousel. Tag what you stored in the app — ribeye steaks, salmon, or chicken breast. DINA holds up to 11 prepared meals deep-frozen at −18 °C.

02

Tap When Ready

Leaving the office at 5:00 PM? Tap your phone to tell DINA what time you plan to eat. The carousel rotates silently, and gravity dispenses tonight’s meal into the dedicated lower defrost drawer.

03

Ready to Cook

Circulating warm air brings food to a tender, chef-grade thaw. When you walk through your front door, tonight’s meal is juicy, perfectly chilled at +3.0 °C, and ready to hit the hot pan.

The Magic Feature

The independent defrost drawer

Engineered so you never have to open the freezer to retrieve tonight’s dinner.

Zero Cold Air Loss

Traditional freezers dump a wave of cold air onto your kitchen floor every time you open them. DINA features an independent sliding bottom drawer. Tonight’s dinner is retrieved with one gentle pull while your bulk frozen food remains undisturbed and sealed at −18 °C.

  • Gentle Convective Thawing: Food is thawed evenly by gentle circulating air — never boiling, never steaming, and zero dried edges.
  • Automatic Safe-Hold: If you are delayed coming home, DINA automatically locks into +3.0 °C refrigeration, keeping dinner fresh for hours.
  • The Halo Light Ring: The handle glows soft amber while active, and turns calm green the moment your dinner is ready.
